# CAMPAIGN ENTITIES REFERENCE
DM-facing. One or two sentences each. Updated as campaign progresses.

---

## PLACES

**The Mersling** — Mid-city tavern frequented by factors, clerks, and off-duty customs officers. Far enough from the harbor that sailors don't drink here; close enough that the city's working layer does. Good place to listen.

**The Anchor & Compass** — Harbor-district tavern, faded sign, door open before midday. Fenn's designated contact point; an unknown man with a broadsheet was watching the exterior when the player passed.

**The Far Pier** — Oldest pier in Liege harbor, darker stone, corroded ironwork. Hound's Dream is moored at its end; the harbor pylon sits on the floor directly beneath it.

**The Harbor Pylon** — Pre-Alliance stone pylon on the harbor floor, roughly 20 feet down beneath the far pier. Unremarkable to anyone without context — harbor workers have seen it for years. Recent scrape marks in the silt and an Acme seal stamp nearby indicate someone dove here recently. The mask responds when the vessel is moored above it.

**Hound's Dream** — Fenn's vessel. 40-45 foot single-masted sloop, dark hull, well-maintained, fast. Moored at the far pier end; departed Liege at first light with the player's crew aboard.

**Farnsworth's House** — Three streets back from the harbor, blue door, unchanged for decades. Family friend and factor; reliable source of Liege street intelligence.

**Chewzwiki's Shop** — Old quarter, floor-to-ceiling shelves, three lamps never all working. Curiosity trader the player has known since childhood; handed over the Acme seal stamp found near the old dock.

**Daring's Room** — Above a cartographer's supply shop in the professional quarter. Charles Daring works here; where the player received the hand-drawn Sinochia margin chart with the pre-Alliance mark.

## PEOPLE

**Admiral Hall** — Distant handler. Orchestrated the player's journey to Liege. Communicates by letter only, plain wax seal, signs with a single initial. Pitches lefty and lies about it.

**Commodore Aldric Fenn (Ret.)** — Hall's man in Liege. Waited three months for an asset worth using. Now aboard with the player, navigating the approach to Wendy Dale's position. Loyal, careful, tests before trusting.

**Wendy Dale** — Culturalist who went to Sinochia and stayed, becoming a working cartographer over time. Has documented the fog margin shifting over the last year. Sinochia doesn't want her leaving with fog charts; Acme wants the records destroyed. Waiting 3 days' sail from Liege.

**Sable Mourne** — Liege harbor archivist, mid-thirties, ink-stained, sleep-deprived. Has been having strange dreams caused by the mask's broadcast near the harbor pylon. Obsessed with pre-Alliance harbor history; has charts showing the pylon's location. Not yet encountered.

**Harwick Dray** — Liege customs officer. Meticulous, not corrupt. Filed a secondary inspection flag on the player's vessel; departed before he could act on it.

**Callo Vesh** — Acme mid-level enforcer in Liege, posing as a Dagnarius factor. Already sent word to Zephyr about the mask. Coordinator, not a fighter — will flee if cornered.

**Mira Solt** — Former Liege harbor pilot, now Acme fixer. Knows every blind spot in the harbor. Her job was to board the player's vessel and plant a tracking chip. Whether she succeeded is unresolved.

**The Brothers Kael** — Two large Acme hired hands posing as cargo handlers. Their job was to separate a player from the group. Clocked by the player on the walk to the docks; likely adjusted their approach.

**Farnsworth** — Family friend and retired factor. Knows everyone in Liege worth knowing. Fed the player and passed on two rumors: fog movement and the retired admiral meeting.

**Chewzwiki** — Curiosity trader, old quarter. Known to the player since childhood. Handed over the Acme seal stamp found near the old dock; no idea what it is.

**Charles Daring** — Working cartographer, former crewmate on the Akamara survey season. Gave the player a hand-drawn copy of the Sinochia margin chart with the pre-Alliance mark included.

**The New Portiguan Woman (Mersling)** — Traveling alone, listening posture, road-worn. Not from Liege. Identity unknown. May reappear.

**The Broadsheet Man (Anchor & Compass)** — Older man, expensive coat maintained carefully, watching the street. Not Fenn. Identity and affiliation unknown.

## OBJECTS

**The Mask (Lignum Vitae artifact)** — Fused to the player's masthead. A Lignum Vitae relic with its own nature — not a container for anything, not a vessel. Cannot be detached. Glows faintly when the vessel is moving; vibrates near pre-Alliance structures. Broadcasts in ways that things in the fog waters notice.

**The Acme Seal Stamp** — Small bronze stamp, circle with a line and lower glyph. Found near the old dock by Chewzwiki; now in the player's pocket. Matches the stamp found in the silt near the harbor pylon. Player has a vague sense of recognition but hasn't placed it yet.

**Daring's Margin Chart** — Hand-drawn copy of the Sinochia fog margin. Shows the current boundary, the previous boundary as a dotted line, and a pre-Alliance mark in older ink at the point the fog moved away from. The approach vector for the next leg.

**The Acme Tracking Chip** — Small arcane resonance device. Mira Solt's job was to plant it in the player's hold. Whether it was planted is unresolved — players won't know unless they search.

**The Brass Compass Rose** — Decorative, no practical use. Bought from Chewzwiki as a matter of habit. Worth exactly what it cost.

## FACTIONS & ENTITIES

**The Recurrent Foundation of the Hand** — Hall's organization. Believes the Alliance is lying to its people about the nature of the world and is working to end that carefully, on its own terms. Operates through trusted assets and indirect communication. Fenn is their man in Liege.

**Acme Corporation / Dr. Zephyr** — Corporate antagonist. Wants the mask, wants Wendy Dale's charts destroyed, wants Dale dead. Has fixers in every major harbor. Zephyr's personal interest is now engaged — next contact will be a proper retrieval team, not fixers.

**R. Asilom** — A very old person. Not connected to the mask. Role and current whereabouts unknown.

**The Axis (Pote / Cush / Qenit)** — Background military pressure. Not directly hunting the player yet. A captured Pote raider in Liege harbor is a slow-burning thread — what it knows about Acme's movements in Axis waters will eventually surface.

**Sinochia** — Alliance member, fog waters, clan-based maritime culture. The fog margin has shifted two to three days of sailing distance in the northern corridors over the last year — whatever is causing it began roughly two years ago. Sinochia considers its fog charts proprietary; Wendy Dale's records are a political problem as well as a dangerous one.
