================================================================================
MODULE: THE FOG BETWEEN
Rescue Wendy Dale. Get her records to safety.
================================================================================

THE HOOK
--------
Commodore Fenn has a job: extract a woman named Wendy Dale from Sinochia
fog waters before Acme Corporation kills her. Dale has a year of records
documenting something wrong happening inside the fog. Acme wants those
records destroyed and Dale with them. Fenn needs a vessel, a crew, and
someone willing to sail into fog that compasses don't work in.

That's the player. The pay is good. The route is one week out, one week
back. Simple enough on paper.

THE STAKES
----------
If the player fails or doesn't go:
  Dale dies. Her records are destroyed. Whatever is causing the fog to
  move goes undocumented. Acme wins this round cleanly.

If the player succeeds:
  Dale and her records reach Ombreval. The Foundation gets the data.
  Whatever Acme is protecting stays exposed.

Secondary stake: the Acme tracking chip (if planted) means a retrieval
team is already moving. Success on the main mission doesn't mean safe.


================================================================================
RUMOR TABLE (roll d4 or pick)
================================================================================

Players can gather these in Liege the day before departure — dockside,
at the Mersling, from Farnsworth or Daring. Not all are true.

1. TRUE — "The fog margin around Sinochia has been moving. Approach lines
   that were reliable last season are inside the fog now. Navigators are
   refusing the northern corridors."
   Source: Farnsworth, Daring, any harbor worker.

2. TRUE — "Acme Corporation has had a man in Liege harbor for six weeks.
   Posing as a Dagnarius factor. Nobody knows what he's watching for."
   Source: Barkeep at the Mersling (Liege intelligence posted a bounty
   on Acme shipping information three days ago — word got around).

3. HALF-TRUE — "The Sinochia fog has a guardian. Sailors who've come back
   from the margin talk about something following their vessel. Most of
   them won't go back."
   True: the Warden exists. False: it doesn't follow vessels out of the
   fog. It stays inside. Sailors who say otherwise are misremembering
   fear as fact.

4. LIE — "Wendy Dale left Sinochia two weeks ago. Someone saw her in
   Dagnarius."
   Source: unknown. This is Acme disinformation — they want the player
   to go to Dagnarius instead of the fog. Dale is still in Sinochia.


================================================================================
LOCATIONS (6)
================================================================================

--- 1. HOUND'S DREAM (Fenn's vessel, the far pier) ---
BOLD / SENSORY / ACTION:
Dark hull, single mast, cleaner than it has any right to be for a vessel
at the far end of the far pier. Smells of tar and old rope. A folding
table at the stern with two cups already set out.

What players can do here:
  - Talk to Fenn. He answers direct questions. He does not volunteer.
  - Inspect the vessel before departure. If they look carefully at their
    own hold, they may find the tracking chip (small bronze disc, warm
    to the touch, faintly humming). If they don't look, they won't find it.
  - Prepare for the voyage: seal charts against moisture, stock supplies,
    ask Fenn what they need to know.

Fenn — wants: to complete the extraction cleanly, on schedule.
Speaks carefully. Will not be rushed. If pushed, he pushes back once
and then goes quiet.

--- 2. THE OPEN WATER (days 1-5, Liege to fog margin) ---
BOLD / SENSORY / ACTION:
Alliance waters thinning to open sea. The Sinochia coastal current
pushes against the hull from the southwest by day three. The horizon
flattens on day five. The light goes wrong — sourceless, no shadows —
before the fog is visible.

What players can do here:
  - Watch Fenn navigate. If they direct Bramble to study his methods,
    Bramble learns the pattern. This matters in the fog.
  - Notice the mask vibrating toward something that is not their heading.
    If they ask Fenn: "It responds to old things. That direction is not
    where we are going." He adjusts course. He watches the masthead.
  - Seal Daring's chart if they haven't. Once inside the fog, the ink
    degrades in two hours if unprotected. Nobody will tell them this.

Encounter — Liege naval patrol (day 1-2):
  Routine clearance. Fenn's papers are good. The vessel is logged and
  cleared. Unremarkable — unless the Harwick Dray customs flag was never
  resolved, in which case the patrol officer hesitates, checks twice,
  and files a secondary note. Not an arrest. A clock.

--- 3. THE FOG MARGIN (day 6, entry) ---
BOLD / SENSORY / ACTION:
Not a wall — a gradient. The horizon disappears over the course of an
hour. The water surface goes flat and grey, like old glass. Smells of
salt and cold stone, like a room closed for a long time. Sound carries
wrong — voices from the bow sound close even when the speaker is far.

What players can do here:
  - See echo vessels: shapes in the fog, ships that passed through
    before them, imperfectly replicated. Wrong rigging. Wrong light
    color. Headings that don't match the wind. Not threats.
  - See their own vessel echoed — slightly wrong, running parallel
    fifty yards to port. If they ask Fenn: "It means we have been noted."
  - Notice the compass drifting. Fenn corrects by dead reckoning and
    water behavior. He does not explain unless asked.

The Warden begins here. See THE WARDEN below.

--- 4. FENN'S LIMIT (fog interior, day 6 midpoint) ---
BOLD / SENSORY / ACTION:
The fog is the same in every direction. No landmarks. The bow barely
visible from the stern. The creak of rigging is very loud. Fenn stops
the vessel.

What happens:
  Fenn's charts end here. He gives the player what he has: a general
  heading (northwest by north), the water temperature trick (warmer
  current on the correct heading), and whatever Bramble has learned.
  Then he waits. He will be here when they return.

  He is exposed. If the tracking chip was planted, an Acme cutter is
  at the margin. Fenn does not know about the chip. Whether the player
  told him is on them.

What players can do here:
  - Use the mask as a negative reference. It pulls toward the pre-Alliance
    mark (northeast). Dale is northwest. Keep the pull to starboard.
    This is not obvious. The player has to figure it out.
  - Direct Bramble to navigate using what he observed. If he watched
    Fenn, he can hold the heading. If he didn't, he can try — but drift
    will be worse.
  - Ask Fenn anything they haven't asked yet. This is the last chance.

--- 5. DALE'S ANCHORAGE (fog interior, ~3 hours northwest of Fenn) ---
BOLD / SENSORY / ACTION:
Three structures on a low rock shelf, barely above the waterline. The
fog is thinner here, close to the rock. One lamp burning in the chart
house window. The mooring line has a specific knot pattern. Dale is
not here.

What players can do here:
  - Read the knot marker. It points to Dale's secondary position,
    ninety minutes further northwest. If they read it, they go direct.
    If they don't, they search — which costs time and may bring the
    Sinochia patrol into the window.
  - Search the anchorage. Dale left a second waterproofed packet here —
    a summary of her findings, one page, in case the primary records
    were lost. The player won't know it's there unless they look.
  - The Warden, if unresolved, followed them here. Dale will see it
    the moment they arrive. Her reaction: "You brought it. That's
    going to be a problem."

--- 6. DALE'S SECONDARY POSITION (ninety minutes further northwest) ---
BOLD / SENSORY / ACTION:
A smaller rock shelf. No structures. Dale in a small tender, waterproofed
case in her lap, watching the fog. She has been here for two days.

Dale — wants: to get out with the records intact. Not to be rescued —
to be extracted. There is a difference and she will make it clear.
Speaks in short sentences. Does not waste words. Looks at the masthead
before she looks at the player.

DALE: "You found the knot." (if they did)
DALE: "You didn't find the knot. How long did that cost us?" (if they didn't)

What players can do here:
  - Ask about the records. She will say: the fog is moving away from
    something. She knows roughly where. She doesn't know what.
  - Ask about the mask. She will say: it's been pulling northeast since
    they entered the fog. That's the direction the fog is moving away from.
  - Ask about the Warden. She will say: "Did you resolve it or is it
    still following you?" If still following: "Then we need to move now."
  - Ask if she knows anyone on the Sinochia patrol. She will say:
    "One person. I would prefer not to need them."


================================================================================
THE WARDEN — PERSISTENT ENCOUNTER (not a room, a presence)
================================================================================

BOLD / SENSORY / ACTION:
A shape slightly denser than the surrounding grey, moving at the edge
of visibility. The water surface disturbs slightly as something large
passes beneath the hull. The mask's vibration changes — less directional,
more reactive, like a dog that has heard something.

What it is: a function of the fog. It maintains the fog, tracks what
moves through it, investigates things that broadcast. The mask broadcasts.
The Warden has been following them since the margin.

What it does: circles. Does not attack. Investigates.

What the player can do — they have to figure this out:
  - Cover the masthead (canvas, rope, anything). Reduces the signal.
    Fenn will hint at this if asked how to make it stop: "Reduce what
    it is following."
  - Hold course and ignore it. It will eventually decide they are
    passing through. Takes twenty minutes of nerve. The hull rocks.
  - Let the mask respond. Don't muffle it, don't run, hold position.
    The mask produces a low tone. The Warden stops circling. This is
    the best outcome — the Warden will recognize the vessel on future
    voyages. Nobody aboard knows this is possible. The player discovers
    it by trying it.

If ignored entirely: it follows them to the anchorage. Dale sees it.
The Sinochia patrol reads the Warden following a vessel as trespass.
The window shrinks.


================================================================================
THE RETURN — WHAT WAITS AT THE MARGIN
================================================================================

Dale navigates the return. She knows these waters. Faster than the
trip in.

IF THE TRACKING CHIP WAS PLANTED:
  An Acme cutter is holding position just outside the fog margin.
  Three crew, two Shinyman Enforcers, one specialist (records handler,
  not a fighter). They want Dale and the records. They will offer to
  let the player go. They will not honor this offer.

  Telegraph the danger: as they approach the margin, the mask's
  vibration changes again — not the Warden, something else. Something
  metal and purposeful. Dale notices it too. "We have company outside."

  The cutter crew is tired and cold — they've been waiting a week.
  The specialist separates easily from the enforcers if the player
  is clever about it.

  Fenn has a Foundation signal flare. He has not mentioned it. He will
  use it without asking if the situation is bad enough. A Foundation
  vessel responds within four hours.

IF THE CHIP WAS NOT PLANTED:
  The margin is clear. Open water. Fenn sets course for the rendezvous.

Either way — Fenn, when Dale boards:
  FENN: "You look well."
  DALE: "I look like someone who spent three days in a fog anchorage.
  Let's go."


================================================================================
FAILING FORWARD
================================================================================

The story does not end if the player fails. It gets more complicated.

FAIL: Player doesn't take the contract.
  Dale waits her full window, then attempts to move on her own. She
  makes it to the margin and is taken by the Acme cutter. The records
  are destroyed. Dale is held. Hall sends another message — this time
  not a contract. A request. The player owes him one.

FAIL: Player loses Dale to the Acme cutter on the return.
  The Sinochia copies exist at the anchorage. The records are not
  fully destroyed. Dale is alive — Acme needs her to explain the
  records before they destroy them. There is a window to get her back.
  It is shorter than the first window.

FAIL: Player navigates badly and misses Dale entirely.
  Dale moves again. She leaves another marker. The player has to
  re-enter the fog. The Sinochia patrol has now logged an unknown
  vessel in their waters. The next entry is not clean.


================================================================================
LOOT / REWARDS
================================================================================

DALE'S SUMMARY PAGE (found at the anchorage if searched):
  One page, waterproofed. A year of fog movement compressed into
  a single diagram. The source marked with an X. No label. No
  coordinates. Just a location relative to the current margin.
  Useful. Incomplete. Enough to know where to go next.

THE WARDEN'S RECOGNITION (if handled well):
  Not an object. A relationship. Future fog voyages: the Warden
  does not investigate the vessel. Passage is faster, quieter,
  and the Sinochia clans notice that the fog does not react to
  this ship the way it reacts to strangers.

DALE'S TRUST (earned over the return voyage):
  If the player handled the voyage competently — found the knot,
  managed the Warden, didn't panic — Dale will open the waterproofed
  case on the return and show them the records. Not describe them.
  Show them. This is the real reward: knowing what Acme is protecting,
  and why the pre-Alliance mark on Daring's chart matters.

  DALE: "The fog isn't moving randomly. It's moving away from something.
  Something that was here before the Alliance, before the fog, before
  any of this. And whatever it is — your mast knows what it is."
