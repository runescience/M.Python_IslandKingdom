2. Scavenging Rules & Mechanics (1st Edition Ruleset Compliance)

Outpost Scavenging Windows

Due to the active fire-arrow trap fuse system running throughout the settlement layout, the crew has a strict, time-sensitive window to locate and dismantle mechanical components before combustion occurs.

  • Time Pools: The party has exactly 3 Scavenging Turns before the fire system triggers full detonation. Each turn represents 10 minutes of active searching, securing, and clearing raw goods.
  • Looting Checks: Attempting to uncover or extract hidden raw items without accidentally snapping the embedded oil-line tripwires requires a Find/Remove Traps thief class check, or an active Dexterity Check (4-in-6 chance on a d6 roll for non-thieves).
  • Failure Impact: A failed check instantly halves the remaining time turns left on the clock and ruins the immediate item stack being pulled out of the cache.

4. D&D 1st Edition Stat Sheets

Vessel Profile: Commercial Merchant Cargo Sloop
Armor Class: 6 (Reinforced Oak)
Movement Speed: 4 Knots Base / 6 Knots Mounted (+50% via Lignum Vitae Masthead)
Crew Capacity: Minimum 12 / Maximum 18
Armament: 2 Mounted Deck Cannons
Ammunition Stocks: Black Powder & Shot for exactly 10 firing attempts total
The Nashua Shipmates (4 Specialized Survivors)

These surviving naval commoners follow standard AD&D 1st Edition Morale rules (Base Morale: 8). They are skilled deckhands but will panic if rations drop to absolute zero or if the vessel takes massive hull trauma.

  • 1. Thorn (Master Ship Carpenter): HD: 1+1 (HP: 7). AC: 9 (Cloth clothes). Weapon: Heavy Adze Tool (1d6 damage). Trait: Can repair exactly 1 point of ship structural hull damage per 4 hours of unhindered work if raw leather straps and tools are available. Friendly, reliable.
  • 2. Bramble (Rigging Hand & Sailmaker): HD: 1 (HP: 5). AC: 8 (Leather Apron). Weapon: Iron Belaying Pin (1d4 damage). Trait: Nimble climber; grants a passive +10% bonus to structural sailing navigation checks regardless of the mask configuration. Quiet, observant.
  • 3. Pip (Cabin Boy / Cook's Mate): HD: 1-1 (HP: 3). AC: 10. Weapon: Rusted Kitchen Cleaver (1d4 damage). Trait: Easily spooked. Must check individual morale at a -2 penalty if water or food provisions completely fail. Nervous talker.
  • 4. Grip (Veteran Cannon Loader): HD: 1+2 (HP: 9). AC: 7 (Boiled Hide Shielding). Weapon: Heavy Treenail Lever (1d6 damage). Trait: Seasoned under fire. Cuts the physical combat loading and preparation time of the sloop's 2 mounted deck cannons in half. Gruff, stoic.
Enemy Combatants: Neutral Alliance Privateers (Crew of 6)

These raiders are intent on intercepting the vessel and securing the outpost secrets before the destruction sequences resolve.

  • 1x Privateer Privateer Captain (Level 3 Fighter): HD: 3 (HP: 22). AC: 4 (Chainmail Coat & Wooden Round Shield). Weapon: Balanced Broadsword (1d8+1 damage). Morale: 10. Tactical trait: Aggressive boarding focus.
  • 5x Neutral Deck Boarders (Level 1 Fighters): HD: 1 (HP: 7 each). AC: 6 (Leather Jerkin & Buckler). Weapon: Heavy Cutlass / Shortsword (1d6 damage). Morale: 8. Tactical trait: Will swing across lines to secure the enemy deck.
  • Surrender Clause: If the Captain is slain and at least 2 boarders fall in battle, the remaining forces lose structural resolve. They will surrender their ship, offering the party a chance to salvage or capture the privateer boat.

5. First Nautical Encounter: The Independent Neutral Kingdom Scout

The Operational Setup: This encounter occurs dynamically on the open water while the crew drops anchor alongside a remote reef-atoll island to gather water or harvest wild plants to offset their food shortages.

The Standoff Context: A lone scout vessel flying the official banners of an Independent Neutral city-state approaches. These scouts have spent the past year navigating the dangerous outer barriers, finding nothing but abandoned ghost villages and empty settlements. They are deeply suspicious, curious, and short on answers.

Tactical Branches:

  • Diplomatic Approach: The Neutrals are not immediately hostile. If the party initiates peaceful talks, they can trade layout rumors regarding Dr. Zephyr's aggressive movements or log data from the burnt outpost in exchange for fresh rations or extra black powder stocks.
  • The Spying Risk: If the players choose to activate the Lignum Vitae mask to outrun them or show off their unnatural 50% class speed bonus, the Neutral captain will immediately realize the party has unlocked a lost foundation technology. The encounter shifts into a tense pursuit where the scouts will track them to steal the secret artifact design.
