Axis Plans Against the Alliance

Chronicle of Known and Suspected Operations — For GM Use Only

The Axis will not start an open war. Every plan here operates below that threshold — through agents, proxies, commerce, corruption, and patience. The goal is not conquest. It is erosion. A weakened Alliance is more useful than a defeated one, because a defeated Alliance becomes a martyr. These plans are long-term, overlapping, and deniable.

Akamara

Alliance capital. Shared ownership. Perpetually gridlocked council. The most surveilled city in the known world — and therefore the most valuable target for infiltration.
Plan 1 — The Empty Seat
Axis lead: Pote  |  Method: Political manipulation
Pote agents have been cultivating a relationship with a minor kingdom's council representative for three years. The goal is not to flip their vote — it is to make them disappear at a critical moment, leaving their seat empty during a vote that requires full quorum. The kingdom they represent will be blamed for the absence. The resulting diplomatic fracture between two Allied kingdoms is the actual objective.
Status: Agent in place. Waiting for the right vote.
Plan 2 — The Grain Futures
Axis lead: Qenit  |  Method: Economic warfare
Qenit is buying grain futures across all ten Allied kingdoms simultaneously through a chain of neutral intermediaries. The goal is not to corner the market — it is to create the appearance of a coming shortage, trigger hoarding behavior, and drive up prices enough to cause civil unrest in the three Allied kingdoms most dependent on imported grain. Akamara's role as the administrative heart means the political fallout lands there first.
Status: Purchasing ongoing. Intermediary chain not yet traced.
Plan 3 — The Royal Inquisitor
Axis lead: Cush  |  Method: Assassination and framing
A Cush sleeper agent embedded in Akamara's administrative class has been tasked with the death of a specific royal inquisitor — one who is close to identifying the Pote grain futures operation. The death must look like an internal Alliance matter. A forged document trail implicating a Liege intelligence officer has already been prepared. The goal is to kill the investigation and fracture Akamara-Liege relations simultaneously.
Status: Inquisitor has been found dead. Investigation quietly closed. Liege has not yet seen the forged documents.

Liege

Largest navy in the Alliance after Akamara. Most stable currency. Merchant houses everywhere. Destroying Liege's financial credibility would destabilize the entire Alliance economy.
Plan 1 — The Counterfeit Currency
Axis lead: Pote  |  Method: Economic warfare
Pote has been producing near-perfect counterfeit Liege currency for two years, slowly introducing it into neutral zone markets through Dagnarius intermediaries. The volume is not yet enough to cause inflation — the goal is to reach a threshold where Liege is forced to publicly acknowledge the counterfeiting, which alone will shake confidence in their currency. The actual economic damage is secondary to the reputational damage.
Status: Counterfeits circulating in Dagnarius and Sender. Not yet detected by Liege.
Plan 2 — The Letter of Marque
Axis lead: Cush  |  Method: Capture and leverage
A Liege captain operating under a letter of marque in Axis-adjacent waters has been captured by Cush. Liege does not know. Cush is not announcing it. The captain is being held in a kontor and slowly turned — given comfort, information, and the gradual suggestion that Liege abandoned them. If successful, the captain will be released back into Liege service as an unwitting asset. If unsuccessful, they will simply disappear.
Status: Captain in Cush custody. Turning process at early stage.
Plan 3 — The Merchant House
Axis lead: Qenit  |  Method: Commercial infiltration
A Qenit-backed shell company has been operating as a legitimate Liege merchant house for four years. It has built genuine trade relationships, a real reputation, and access to Liege port manifests. The goal is not profit — it is to map every Allied naval resupply route, identify which ports are most dependent on which supply chains, and deliver that intelligence to Qenit fleet command. The house will be burned when the map is complete.
Status: House operational. Map approximately 70% complete.

Manchu Empire

300 million population. Despot king. Difficult bureaucracy. Self-sufficient economy. Hard to infiltrate directly — the Axis approach is to use Manchu's own rivalry with Sinochia as a weapon.
Plan 1 — The Forged Dispatch
Axis lead: Pote  |  Method: Disinformation
Pote forgers have produced a convincing Sinochia diplomatic dispatch — addressed to a neutral kingdom — that implies Sinochia is negotiating a separate trade agreement that would undercut Manchu's internal market. The document is false. The goal is to place it where Manchu intelligence will find it, deepen the Manchu-Sinochia rift, and consume both kingdoms' political energy in a dispute that benefits no one in the Alliance.
Status: Document prepared. Delivery method not yet determined.
Plan 2 — The Bureaucratic Delay
Axis lead: Qenit  |  Method: Administrative sabotage
Qenit has placed an agent inside Manchu's civil service — not a spy, but a genuine bureaucrat who has risen through legitimate means over eight years. Their task is not to steal documents but to delay them. Specific military supply requisitions, fleet repair authorizations, and inter-kingdom trade licenses are being held in procedural limbo for weeks longer than necessary. The cumulative effect on Manchu's military readiness is significant and completely invisible.
Status: Agent in place and active. Delays attributed to normal bureaucratic inefficiency.
Plan 3 — The King's Portrait
Axis lead: Cush  |  Method: Assassination — long preparation
The despot king's requirement that his portrait be displayed everywhere creates a supply chain of artists, printers, and distributors with access to every public building in Manchu. Cush has been mapping this supply chain for two years. The plan is not yet active — it is a contingency. If the Axis ever decides Manchu's king needs to die, the infrastructure to deliver something lethal through the portrait distribution network already exists.
Status: Mapping complete. Plan dormant. Activation requires Axis high command approval.

Sinochia

600 million population. Supernatural fog. Clan networks in every port. The fog makes direct military action impossible — the Axis approach is to attack the trade networks that are Sinochia's actual power base.
Plan 1 — The Fog Chart
Axis lead: Qenit  |  Method: Intelligence acquisition
Qenit has been collecting every fragment of navigational data on Sinochia's fog waters for a decade — from captured sailors, bribed merchants, and intercepted charts. No single fragment is enough. Together they are building a partial map. The goal is not to navigate the fog perfectly — it is to navigate it well enough to strike one target, once, and withdraw. A single successful Axis incursion into Sinochia's fog waters would shatter the psychological invulnerability the fog provides.
Status: Map fragments being assembled. Estimated 40% complete. Several sources have gone silent.
Plan 2 — The Clan Debt
Axis lead: Pote  |  Method: Financial leverage
Pote has extended generous credit terms to three minor Sinochia trading clans through neutral intermediaries, allowing them to expand aggressively into new markets. The clans do not know who their creditor ultimately is. When the debts are called in simultaneously — at a moment of Pote's choosing — the clans will face ruin unless they provide something in return. What Pote wants is access to clan shipping manifests and port schedules for six months.
Status: Debts held. Clans currently profitable. Pote waiting for optimal moment to call them in.
Plan 3 — The Disputed Island
Axis lead: Cush  |  Method: Proxy conflict
The island disputed between Sinochia and Manchu is of no strategic value to Cush. But the dispute is. Cush has been quietly supplying a minor Manchu border faction with navigational intelligence about the island's approaches — not enough to resolve the dispute, but enough to encourage Manchu to press its claim more aggressively. Every escalation between Manchu and Sinochia over the island consumes Alliance council time, strains the two kingdoms' relationship, and distracts both from Axis activity elsewhere.
Status: Intelligence deliveries ongoing. Manchu faction unaware of Cush involvement.

New Portigua

Nine island territories. Unmatched cartographic knowledge. The charts New Portigua holds are worth more than their entire fleet. Destroying their navigational monopoly would reshape the balance of power at sea.
Plan 1 — The Chart Theft
Axis lead: Pote  |  Method: Espionage
New Portigua's cartographic archive is the most valuable single collection of documents in the known world. Pote has spent three years placing an agent inside the archive's administrative staff — not as a thief, but as a copyist. The agent is not stealing charts. They are making perfect copies, one section at a time, and passing them out in pieces small enough to be concealed. No single piece is useful. The complete set would give Pote navigational access to every sea lane New Portigua has ever charted.
Status: Agent active. Approximately one third of priority charts copied.
Plan 2 — The Ghost Island Intercept
Axis lead: Qenit  |  Method: Intelligence denial
New Portigua has more Ghost Island sightings than any other kingdom and refuses to share them. Qenit believes the Ghost Island is real, fixed, and navigable — and that New Portigua already knows where it is. Qenit agents have been following New Portiguan patrol vessels, intercepting their amended logs, and bribing harbor masters for copies of confiscated charts. The goal is to find the Ghost Island before New Portigua officially acknowledges it — and claim it first.
Status: Three partial sighting logs acquired. Location still unconfirmed.
Plan 3 — The Colonial Fracture
Axis lead: Cush  |  Method: Destabilization of island territories
New Portigua's nine island territories are spread thin and governed at a distance. Cush has identified two territories where local populations have historical grievances against New Portigua's colonial administration. Cush agents are not fomenting rebellion — they are funding it quietly, through third parties, in ways that cannot be traced. The goal is to force New Portigua to divert naval resources from Alliance patrol duties to internal suppression, reducing their presence on the Axis frontier.
Status: Funding active in two territories. Unrest growing. New Portigua has not yet connected the incidents.

NewFoundland / Akira

Builds the finest ships in the Alliance. Vast cold-water territories. If the Axis can degrade NewFoundland's shipbuilding capacity, every Allied navy suffers within a generation.
Plan 1 — The Timber Blight
Axis lead: Qenit  |  Method: Ecological sabotage
NewFoundland's shipbuilding depends on specific old-growth timber found only in its northern forests. Qenit agents have introduced a slow-acting fungal blight into three of the most productive logging regions — not enough to cause immediate alarm, but enough to reduce timber yield over five to ten years. By the time the cause is identified, a generation of shipbuilding capacity will have been quietly degraded. The blight looks entirely natural.
Status: Blight introduced. Early signs appearing. Not yet attributed to sabotage.
Plan 2 — The Guild Master
Axis lead: Pote  |  Method: Corruption
NewFoundland's thing council includes guild masters whose votes determine shipbuilding contracts and resource allocation. Pote has identified one guild master with significant personal debt — not to Pote, but to a Dagnarius trading house that Pote quietly controls. The debt will be called in at a moment of Pote's choosing, with a simple offer: vote against the next major Alliance naval contract renewal, or face ruin. The guild master does not need to know who is really asking.
Status: Debt held. Guild master unaware. Vote opportunity being monitored.
Plan 3 — The Hull Design
Axis lead: Cush  |  Method: Intellectual theft
NewFoundland's cold-water hull designs are what make their ships survivable in conditions that sink everyone else. Cush wants those designs. An agent posing as a foreign apprentice has spent two years in a NewFoundland shipyard, learning the craft legitimately. They are not yet ready to leave — the most critical design knowledge is held by master craftspeople who share it only with trusted journeymen. The agent is still building that trust.
Status: Agent in place. Trust-building ongoing. Estimated one to two years from extraction.

Tokyoshima

Financial hub. Merchant banks hold debt from half the Alliance. Razor-sharp social hierarchy. The Axis does not need to attack Tokyoshima militarily — they need to make it financially unreliable.
Plan 1 — The Debt Ledger
Axis lead: Pote  |  Method: Financial intelligence
Tokyoshima's merchant banks hold debt instruments from kingdoms across the Alliance. If Pote can obtain a complete picture of who owes what to whom, they can identify which Allied kingdoms are most financially vulnerable and time economic pressure on those kingdoms to coincide with political crises. A Pote agent has been working as a mid-level bank clerk for eighteen months, copying ledger entries in fragments small enough to be memorized rather than written down.
Status: Intelligence gathering ongoing. Partial ledger picture assembled.
Plan 2 — The Protocol Breach
Axis lead: Cush  |  Method: Social sabotage
Tokyoshima's culture is built on correct form — outsiders who violate protocol are frozen out permanently. Cush has been studying Tokyoshima's social codes for years and has prepared a scenario in which a prominent Alliance diplomat — framed through forged correspondence — commits a serious and public protocol violation at a Tokyoshima court function. The goal is to permanently damage that diplomat's ability to operate in Tokyoshima and, by extension, their kingdom's relationship with the financial hub.
Status: Scenario prepared. Target diplomat identified. Waiting for a suitable court function.
Plan 3 — The Bank Run
Axis lead: Qenit  |  Method: Economic warfare — contingency
Qenit has been quietly accumulating Tokyoshima debt instruments purchased through neutral intermediaries over seven years. They do not yet hold enough to trigger a crisis. The plan is a contingency: if the Axis ever needs to destabilize the Alliance at a critical moment, calling in a significant portion of Tokyoshima's outstanding debt simultaneously — combined with a coordinated rumor campaign about the bank's solvency — could trigger a financial panic that would consume the Alliance's attention for months.
Status: Debt accumulation ongoing. Approximately 15% of threshold required. Plan dormant.

Ombreval / Dark Valley

Cities of refuge. The Nameless Legion. Mountain-ringed and labyrinthine. The Axis hates Ombreval specifically — it shelters people the Axis wants back, and its Legion fights under no flag they can officially protest.
Plan 1 — The Planted Petitioner
Axis lead: Pote  |  Method: Infiltration
Pote has trained an agent to present a convincing case to the Elders of the Threshold and be granted legitimate refuge. Once inside, the agent's task is not violence — it is to map the labyrinth streets, identify the locations of specific high-value refugees the Axis wants recovered, and pass that information out through a dead drop near the mountain passes. The agent has been in place for four months and has not yet been detected.
Status: Agent inside Ombreval. Mapping ongoing. Three targets identified.
Plan 2 — The Legion Contract
Axis lead: Cush  |  Method: Proxy manipulation
The Nameless Legion takes mercenary contracts abroad. Cush has arranged, through three layers of intermediaries, for a contract to be offered to a Legion company that would place them in a specific location at a specific time — where a Cush ambush is waiting. The goal is not to destroy the company but to capture Legionnaires, strip their anonymity, and identify which of them are former Axis citizens who fled. Those individuals will be publicly named and their crimes announced, forcing Ombreval into a diplomatic confrontation.
Status: Contract offer being routed. Legion company not yet selected.
Plan 3 — The Forged Refusal
Axis lead: Qenit  |  Method: Disinformation
Ombreval's moral authority rests on the Elders of the Threshold being incorruptible. Qenit forgers have produced documents purporting to show that a specific Elder accepted a bribe to refuse a petitioner — a petitioner who subsequently died. The documents are false. The goal is to place them where an Alliance journalist or council investigator will find them, triggering a public inquiry that consumes Ombreval's political capital and forces the crown to defend its most sacred institution.
Status: Documents prepared. Distribution method under consideration.

Khazan Steppe Empire

Enormous army. Vast unmapped territories. Nomadic structure makes it hard to strike at a center. The Axis approach is to attack the oasis cities — the fixed points that hold the empire together.
Plan 1 — The Kurgan
Axis lead: Qenit  |  Method: Cultural desecration and provocation
Qenit agents have opened and looted a kurgan near a major Khazani trade road — not for the contents, but for the reaction. The balbals have been moved and defaced. The goal is to provoke a Khazani military response against the wrong target — a neutral kingdom whose territory borders the kurgan site will be blamed through planted evidence. If successful, Khazan will spend military and diplomatic resources on a confrontation with a neutral kingdom rather than watching the Axis frontier.
Status: Kurgan opened. Evidence planted. Khazani response being monitored.
Plan 2 — The River Quarter
Axis lead: Pote  |  Method: Contamination
Khazan's oasis cities depend on river water for everything — drinking, agriculture, the moats that protect their earthen ramparts. Pote has been studying the river systems feeding the largest oasis city for two years. The plan is to introduce a slow-acting contaminant upstream — not lethal, but enough to cause widespread illness and force the city to seal its river quarter while the source is investigated. The timing is intended to coincide with the winter nomad influx, when the city's population is at its highest.
Status: River system mapped. Contaminant identified. Delivery method not yet finalized.
Plan 3 — The Eastern General
Axis lead: Cush  |  Method: Defection and leverage
A Khazani general commanding the eastern frontier has been in contact with Cush agents for six months. He has not defected — he has been offered terms and is considering them. What Cush wants is not military intelligence but a single act: the general goes silent at a critical moment, failing to respond to a Khagan dispatch, creating the appearance of insubordination or worse. The resulting internal crisis within Khazan's military command structure would take months to resolve.
Status: General in contact. Terms under negotiation. Has not yet committed.

EuroPublic: M/Mont Blanc & M/Ebon Towers

No military. No resource base. Entirely dependent on Alliance administrative fees. Produces the legal framework the entire Alliance runs on. Corrupting EuroPublic corrupts everything downstream.
Plan 1 — The Commissioner
Axis lead: Pote  |  Method: Corruption
A EuroPublic commissioner has been meeting privately with a Pote-connected envoy for four months. The commissioner is not ideologically aligned with the Axis — they are financially compromised. Pote holds evidence of a personal scandal that would end their career. In exchange for silence, the commissioner has been asked to do one thing: ensure that a specific trade regulation — one that would restrict Axis-adjacent neutral ports — never makes it out of committee. They have complied twice already.
Status: Commissioner compromised and active. Regulation stalled indefinitely.
Plan 2 — The Dead Vote
Axis lead: Qenit  |  Method: Institutional fraud
A EuroPublic commissioner died three weeks ago. Their seat is casting proxy votes. Qenit is responsible — an agent with access to the commission's administrative process has been casting the votes using the dead commissioner's seal, which was never recovered after their death. The votes have been correct on every minor matter, building a record of reliability. When a critical vote arrives, the proxy will cast against Alliance interests. By the time the fraud is discovered, the vote will have passed.
Status: Proxy active. Critical vote not yet identified. Agent maintaining cover.
Plan 3 — The Archive
Axis lead: Cush  |  Method: Historical intelligence
The sealed chamber in the Mont Blanc archive predates the EuroPublic by two centuries. Cush believes it contains the original draft of the Alliance founding charter — including clauses that were removed before ratification. If those clauses reveal internal contradictions or compromises that were deliberately hidden, making them public could challenge the legal legitimacy of the Alliance's founding documents. Cush has been trying to obtain the key or a copy of the chamber's contents for three years.
Status: Two attempts to access the chamber have failed. A third approach is being planned.
