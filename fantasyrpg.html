Sci-Fi to Fantasy RPG Adventure Conversion

This framework translates a classic Traveller-style sci-fi space encounter into a boots-on-the-ground, classic high-fantasy adventure while preserving player agency and eliminating the narrative railroad.

The Core Conversion Matrix

Traveller Sci-Fi Concept Classic Fantasy Equivalent
Liege World / Planet B Royal Capital / Allied Foreign Kingdom
Deep Space (The Void) The Great Ashen Wastes (Deadly, isolated borderlands)
Impulse Drive / Spaceship Sand-Skiff / Behemoth-Caravan (Wind-elemental powered runner)
Diplomatic Pouch / Stasis Pack Sealed Royal Scroll-Case / Iron Lockbox (Wax-stamped)
Planet A Carrier / Star Fortress The Mobile Fortress-Crawler (Moving stone castle on iron tracks)
Space Pirates / Raiders Wasteland Skirmishers (Mounted on giant hunting lizards)
The Secret Twist: The sealed container isn't just carrying documents; it holds a volatile, living magical creature that reacts to the journey.

Step-by-Step Adventure Guide & Player Options

Step 1: The Recruitment & Interception

The Situation: The players are recruited by their King to move a sealed diplomatic scroll-case on a two-week journey across the deadly Ashen Wastes using an elemental Sand-Skiff. Four days into the trek, a massive Mobile Fortress-Crawler operated by border-wardens blocks their path. They hail the players via signaling mirrors, demanding to validate credentials and warning of raiders ahead.

Player Options (Bypassing the Railroad):
  • Comply: Park the Sand-Skiff inside the fortress’s lower gates. They gain safety and intelligence but must surrender local operational control.
  • Flee/Evade: Attempt to outrun or navigate around the massive, slow moving fortress. This triggers a chase sequence through jagged rocky canyons where the massive crawler cannot follow.
  • Bluff: Use royal credentials and communication mirrors to convince the Warden Captain that stopping voids their strict diplomatic timeline, forcing a tense standoff or a hard choice for the Wardens.

Step 2: Isolation & The Spies

The Situation: Inside the fortress, the players are escorted to a secure holding wing or a private tavern room while their papers are processed. While stuck there, clandestine enemy agents/spies infiltrate the lower docking bay to inspect and tamper with their Sand-Skiff, looking for the secret pouch.

Player Options (Bypassing the Railroad):

Instead of locking the players behind cutscene bars, let them discover the threat:

  • Investigate: A passive perception check or a nervous servant tips them off that "inspectors" just headed down to their vehicle.
  • Infiltrate: Players can use stealth, illusions, or magic (like Invisibility) to sneak back to the docks, allowing them to ambush the spies mid-inspection.
  • Leverage: Confront the Warden Captain directly about suspicious security movements, turning the fortress guards against the hidden third-party spies.

Step 3: The Ambush & Chaos

The Situation: Suddenly, a massive explosion rocks the Mobile Fortress. Wasteland raiders launch a coordinated assault, breaching the walls with heavy war beasts. Fire rolls down the hallways, creating total chaos as the structure begins to fail.

Player Options (Bypassing the Railroad):

Give the players competing operational priorities in the crisis:

  • Priority A (The Cargo): Disregard the fortress entirely. Fight a direct, bloody gauntlet through invading raiders straight to the lower bay to secure the skiff and prepare for exit.
  • Priority B (The Heroes): The Warden Captain or a key NPC is cornered by raiders nearby. Defending them slows the escape but secures valuable political favors or a battlefield ally.
  • Priority C (The Intel): The explosion blew open the spies' locked command room. The players can detour to steal the enemy's correspondence, learning exactly why their diplomatic cargo is being targeted.

Step 4: The Breakout & Wasteland Firefight

The Situation: The players reach their Sand-Skiff, but the main fortress bay doors are jammed or blocked. They must break out into the dark wasteland while fast-moving raider skirmishers pursue them through the night.

Player Options (Bypassing the Railroad):
  • The Direct Ram: Spin up the wind-elemental engines to maximum power and use the skiff's reinforced hull to smash through the damaged ironwood bay gates, launching directly into the sand dunes.
  • The Arcane Firefight: Man the skiff's mounted heavy weapons. Players use heavy ballistas firing explosive oil-tipped bolts and fixed arcane cannons to blast elemental lightning into the dark at pursuing lizard-riders.
  • The Tactical Counter-Attack: Instead of running into the dark immediately, use the skiff's mounted artillery to help the fortress defenders break the back of the raider siege lines, changing the geopolitical landscape of the wastes.

The GM Golden Rule for Sandbox Isolation

To keep this feeling like an open-ended, dangerous Traveller sandbox rather than a scripted fantasy module, do not write a script of events. Instead, track the Factions and Motivations present in the area:

  • The Border Wardens: Want to strictly enforce territorial security, protect their fortress, and eliminate raider threats.
  • The Spies: Want to identify and steal or confirm the contents of the pouch without starting an open war with the wardens.
  • The Wasteland Raiders: Want to destroy the moving fortress, pillage its supplies, and claim any high-value cargo navigating the wastes.
  • The Secret Cargo: Wants to escape its container, reacting to the stress, magic, and combat sounds outside by leaking its own energy or projecting telepathic distress calls.

Set these forces in motion, present the initial obstacle, and let your players' choices determine who survives the night in the deep wilds.
