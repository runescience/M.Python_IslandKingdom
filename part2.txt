================================================================================
CAMPAIGN PART 2: AFTER THE DELIVERY — THE LIEGE COMPLICATION
================================================================================

SITUATION SUMMARY
-----------------
The players have successfully delivered Admiral Hall's sealed diplomatic pouch
to Liege (03,04). The job is done. But arriving in Liege with a foreign-crested
sloop, a Lignum Vitae artifact fused to the masthead, and knowledge of the
Great Lie makes them immediately dangerous to the status quo — and immediately
interesting to every faction watching the docks.

The moment they make port, the clock starts ticking on three separate problems
converging on them at once.


================================================================================
WHAT THE ADVERSARIES ARE DOING WHILE THE PLAYERS SETTLE IN
================================================================================

--- DR. ZEPHYR / ACME CORPORATION ---

Acme has had eyes on Liege for months. Per the Kingdom Compendium, Acme
already has a presence two blocks from the Alliance council chambers in Akamara
(03,03), and Liege intelligence is actively paying double rate for information
on Acme's shipping routes. That means Acme knows Liege is watching them — and
they've been careful.

What Acme is doing RIGHT NOW:
  - A mid-level Acme enforcer named CALLO VESH has been stationed in Liege
    harbor for six weeks, posing as a Dagnarius factor. His actual job: watch
    for anything unusual arriving from the outer reefs. The players' sloop —
    reinforced hull, two mounted cannons, moving at impossible speed — just
    lit up every flag he has.
  - Callo has already sent a carrier pigeon to Zephyr. The message: "The mask
    is in Liege harbor. Two primary operators. Unknown affiliation."
  - Zephyr's response will arrive within 48 hours: "Acquire the vessel. If
    the artifact cannot be separated, acquire the operators. Do not damage
    the masthead."
  - Acme has three hired blades already in Liege — not soldiers, fixers. They
    will begin surveillance immediately and move to action once Zephyr's
    confirmation arrives.

The players have roughly 2 days before Acme shifts from watching to acting.

--- ADMIRAL HALL / THE RECURRENT FOUNDATION OF THE HAND ---

Hall orchestrated this entire journey. He knew the pouch would get the players
to Liege. What he didn't tell them: the pouch contained more than peace
treaties. It contained a sealed secondary document — a formal request to Liege
intelligence to extend the Foundation's protection network into Liege's outer
island possessions.

Hall's man in Liege is a retired naval officer named COMMODORE ALDRIC FENN.
Fenn is old, careful, and deeply loyal to the Foundation. He has been waiting
for this delivery for three months.

What Fenn is doing RIGHT NOW:
  - He received word the players arrived. He is watching them from a distance,
    assessing whether they are ready to be brought further into the Foundation's
    confidence.
  - He will not approach them directly. Instead, he will arrange for a
    seemingly random encounter — a dockside merchant who "happens" to mention
    that a man named Fenn would like to buy them a drink at a specific tavern
    at a specific time.
  - If the players show up, Fenn will offer them a new contract: escort a
    Foundation courier through Sinochia fog waters to a location he won't
    name until they're underway. Pay is generous. The real purpose is to test
    whether the players can be trusted with Foundation-level operations.
  - If the players don't show up, Fenn will try once more, then write them
    off as uncontrollable assets and report back to Hall accordingly.

--- R. ASILOM (THE ENTITY) ---

The Lignum Vitae mask is still fused to the masthead. R. Asilom's fragments
are still in it. Being this close to a major Alliance port — with its ancient
harbor infrastructure, its deep-water moorings, its centuries-old stone
foundations — is activating something in the entity.

What R. Asilom is doing RIGHT NOW:
  - The mask has begun vibrating at irregular intervals, always when pointed
    toward the old harbor district. There is something buried under Liege's
    oldest dock — a sealed stone chamber, pre-Alliance construction, that the
    city's founders built over and forgot.
  - The entity is not strong enough to act directly. But it is broadcasting.
    Anyone sensitive to arcane energy in Liege — and there are a few — will
    have started having strange dreams in the last 48 hours. Dreams of water,
    of gates, of a melody they can't quite place.
  - One of those dreamers is a Liege harbor archivist named SABLE MOURNE, who
    has been quietly excavating the old harbor records for years, convinced
    something important is buried under the oldest dock. She will find the
    players. She doesn't know why. She just knows the dreams pointed her here.

--- THE AXIS (BACKGROUND PRESSURE) ---

Per the campaign map, Pote (04,06), Cush (03,07), and Qenit (03,08) form a
frontline armada between the players and any route back toward the frontier.
While the Axis isn't directly hunting the players yet, a Pote raider was
recently captured and brought to Liege harbor — and hasn't been publicly
announced (per the Liege rumor table).

What this means for the players:
  - Liege is quietly on a war footing. Extra naval patrols. Tighter paperwork
    checks. A customs officer who is "unusually thorough" (per the encounter
    table) will board their sloop within 24 hours of arrival.
  - The captured Pote raider is being held in a secure facility two streets
    from the harbor. Someone on that ship knows something about Acme's
    movements in Axis waters. Liege intelligence wants that information before
    Acme finds out the raider was taken.


================================================================================
THE NEXT ADVENTURE: "THE LIEGE COMPLICATION"
================================================================================

STRUCTURE: Three interlocking problems that the players must navigate
simultaneously, each with its own clock.

--- PROBLEM 1: THE CUSTOMS INSPECTION (Clock: 24 hours) ---

A Liege customs officer named HARWICK DRAY boards the sloop within a day of
arrival. He is meticulous, polite, and finds something he shouldn't: the
unrecognized country crests on the uniforms the players may still have from
the vacant island outpost (Chapter 1). He doesn't arrest them — he doesn't
have grounds yet — but he files a report and flags the vessel for secondary
inspection in 48 hours.

The players have two days to either:
  A) Get the uniforms off the ship and establish a cover story before the
     secondary inspection.
  B) Find someone with enough authority to quash the flag (Fenn can do this,
     if they've met him).
  C) Leave port before the 48 hours are up — which will itself raise flags
     and put them on a watch list.

If they fail all three options, they get pulled in for formal questioning by
Liege intelligence. This isn't necessarily fatal — it could become a forced
introduction to the Foundation's Liege network — but it costs them time and
exposes the mask.

--- PROBLEM 2: ACME MOVES (Clock: 48 hours) ---

Callo Vesh's confirmation arrives. Acme's three fixers begin active
surveillance, then move to acquire.

The fixers are not soldiers. They are professionals:
  - MIRA SOLT: A former Liege harbor pilot. Knows every dock, every alley,
    every blind spot in the harbor district. Will attempt to get aboard the
    sloop while the players are ashore.
  - THE BROTHERS KAEL: Two large men who work as legitimate cargo handlers
    by day. Their job is to separate one player from the group and offer a
    "business proposition" — meaning a knife and a quiet room.
  - CALLO VESH himself: Will not act directly. He is the coordinator. If the
    players identify him, he will attempt to flee and warn Zephyr.

The players can:
  A) Detect the surveillance early (Bramble's passive +10% navigation bonus
     applies to noticing things that are wrong on a dock) and turn the tables.
  B) Let it play out and deal with the confrontation when it comes.
  C) Use the situation to feed Acme false information about where they're
     headed next.

If Mira Solt gets aboard the sloop, she will not steal the mask — she can't
detach it. But she will plant a tracking device (a small arcane resonance
chip, Acme technology) in the hold. The players won't know it's there unless
they search carefully.

--- PROBLEM 3: SABLE MOURNE AND THE BURIED GATE (Clock: Flexible) ---

The harbor archivist finds the players. She is nervous, sleep-deprived, and
carrying a rolled-up set of pre-Alliance harbor charts that she has been
collecting for years. She doesn't know what she's looking for. She just knows
the players' ship is connected to whatever is under the old dock.

If the players engage with her:
  - She shows them the charts. The oldest dock in Liege was built over a
    sealed stone chamber that predates the Alliance by at least 400 years.
    The chamber is marked on the original survey maps with a symbol the
    players may recognize: the same glyph that appeared on the Emeritus
    Colony databanks from Chapter 4.
  - The mask vibrates violently when pointed at the dock. R. Asilom is
    trying to reach whatever is down there.
  - Getting into the chamber requires either bribing the dock authority
    (expensive), forging a survey permit (risky), or going in at night
    through the water (dangerous, but the players have diving bladders).

What's in the chamber:
  - A dormant transit gate. Not active. Not even close to active. But intact.
    The first one the players have seen in person.
  - A stone inscription in a language that matches fragments from the Emeritus
    databanks. It describes the gate network and names the nearest active
    node: somewhere in the Ruined Charts (R242, 03,06).
  - Evidence that someone else has been here recently. Boot prints in the
    dust. A Acme Corporation seal stamp, dropped and forgotten.

This is the hook for the next leg of the journey: the Ruined Charts, and
whatever Acme is doing there.


================================================================================
THE NEXT LEG: ROUTE OPTIONS AND WHAT WAITS THERE
================================================================================

The players now have:
  - The global map (from Chapter 4, either extracted or imprinted by the Gaxo)
  - Knowledge of the buried gate under Liege's old dock
  - An inscription pointing toward R242 (03,06) in the Ruined Charts
  - Acme actively hunting them
  - A potential Foundation contact in Fenn (if they took the meeting)
  - A customs flag that will become a formal investigation if they stay

Their logical next move is to leave Liege. The question is which direction.

ROUTE A: NORTH THROUGH DAGNARIUS (06,03)
  - Safest from Alliance patrols. No paperwork required.
  - Acme has agents in Dagnarius (per the Kingdom Compendium). The tracking
    chip (if planted) will lead Callo Vesh's report directly to them.
  - Fenn's courier contract, if accepted, runs through Sinochia fog waters —
    which means passing near Dagnarius anyway.
  - Encounter risk: Moderate. Acme presence, but no Axis.

ROUTE B: THROUGH THE RUINED CHARTS DIRECTLY (R242, 03,06)
  - The inscription points here. The mask will likely react strongly.
  - Nobody sails the Ruined Charts willingly. Wild magic anomalies, sea
    monsters, and the wreckage of whatever happened 300 years ago.
  - Acme has been here recently. Whatever they found, they didn't announce it.
  - Encounter risk: High. Unknown. Potentially the most important location
    in the campaign so far.

ROUTE C: BACK TOWARD THE FRONTIER (SOUTH)
  - The players know the frontier. They have allies there (the Gaxo, if they
    took Route B in Chapter 4; the Sundown Atoll marshal).
  - The Axis frontline blocks direct southern passage. They would need to
    thread through Bartog (05,04) or Sender (05,06) buffer zones.
  - Encounter risk: High from Axis patrols. Moderate from privateers.


================================================================================
ADVERSARY MOVEMENTS DURING THE NEXT LEG (WHEREVER THEY GO)
================================================================================

ACME:
  - If the tracking chip was planted, Callo Vesh will file a route report
    within 12 hours of the players leaving port. Acme will have a vessel
    positioned to intercept within 3-4 days of sailing.
  - If the chip was not planted (players found and removed it, or Mira Solt
    was caught), Acme loses the trail temporarily. They will pick it up again
    at the next port the players enter — Acme has eyes in every major harbor.
  - Zephyr's personal interest is now engaged. He will not send fixers again.
    He will send a proper retrieval team: a fast Acme cutter with six
    Shinyman Enforcers and a specialist whose job is to detach the mask from
    the masthead by any means necessary.

ADMIRAL HALL / FENN:
  - If the players took Fenn's contract, they have Foundation cover for their
    movements. Fenn will quietly suppress the customs flag and ensure their
    departure from Liege goes smoothly.
  - If they didn't take the contract, Hall will hear about the Liege visit
    through his own channels. He will be frustrated but not surprised. He
    will make one more attempt to reach the players — this time through a
    message left at a neutral port (Dagnarius or Bartog), signed only with
    the Foundation's symbol.

R. ASILOM:
  - The entity now knows there is a dormant gate in Liege. It cannot act on
    this directly. But it will begin subtly influencing the mask's navigation
    pull — steering the players toward R242 whether they intend to go there
    or not. The mask's compass interference will become more pronounced the
    closer they get to the Ruined Charts.
  - If the players resist the pull long enough, the entity will attempt
    something more direct: a dream-intrusion, similar to the Gaxo's
    dream-weaving but corrupted. The players will wake from it unsettled,
    with fragmented images of a gate opening and something vast moving
    through it.

THE AXIS:
  - The captured Pote raider in Liege harbor is a ticking clock. If the
    players leave without engaging with that situation, Liege intelligence
    will eventually extract the information themselves — and one piece of
    that information is that Acme has been running supply lines through
    Pote-controlled waters. This will accelerate the Alliance's investigation
    of Acme, which is good for the players long-term but creates short-term
    chaos in every port they visit.


================================================================================
KEY NPCs INTRODUCED THIS CHAPTER
================================================================================

CALLO VESH
  Role: Acme mid-level enforcer / harbor spy
  Appearance: Heavyset, well-dressed, always has a ledger in hand. Looks
              exactly like a Dagnarius factor.
  Motivation: Career advancement within Acme. Not a true believer — just
              ambitious. Can be bribed or turned if the players are clever.
  Stat Block (1st Ed): HD 2+2 (HP 13). AC 7 (leather under merchant clothes).
              THAC0 18. Weapon: concealed short sword (1d6). Special: if
              cornered, will attempt to flee and warn Acme rather than fight.

COMMODORE ALDRIC FENN (RETIRED)
  Role: Foundation contact in Liege
  Appearance: Old, weathered, moves like a man who spent forty years at sea.
              Drinks slowly. Watches everything.
  Motivation: Loyalty to the Foundation and to Hall. Genuinely believes the
              Great Lie needs to end — carefully, on the Foundation's terms.
  Note: Will not reveal Foundation secrets until the players have earned
        significant trust. Will test them repeatedly before doing so.

SABLE MOURNE
  Role: Liege harbor archivist / accidental ally
  Appearance: Mid-thirties, ink-stained fingers, carries too many rolled
              charts. Looks perpetually sleep-deprived.
  Motivation: Academic obsession with pre-Alliance harbor history. Has no
              idea she's been dream-influenced by R. Asilom. Genuinely just
              wants to know what's under the dock.
  Note: Useful as a recurring NPC. She has access to Liege's historical
        records and can provide research support for future investigations
        into the gate network.

HARWICK DRAY
  Role: Liege customs officer
  Appearance: Precise, formal, never raises his voice.
  Motivation: Does his job. That's it. Not corrupt, not cruel, just thorough.
  Note: Can become an ally if the players handle the inspection honestly and
        with the right paperwork. Can become a serious obstacle if they lie
        badly.

SESSION HOOKS (PICK ONE TO OPEN WITH)
================================================================================

  Open with Harwick Dray stepping aboard the sloop, ledger in hand, politely
  asking to see their transit papers. The uniforms are visible in the hold.

HOOK B — THE DREAM:
  Open with one player waking from a vivid, disturbing dream: a stone gate
  underwater, a melody (Werewolves of London, distorted), and a voice that
  isn't quite human saying "you are close." The mask is glowing faintly.

HOOK C — THE APPROACH:
  Open with a dockside merchant pressing a folded note into the lead player's
  hand as they walk the harbor. The note reads: "The Anchor & Compass. Tonight.
  Ask for Fenn. Come alone or don't come." No signature.

HOOK D — THE TAIL:
  Open with Bramble quietly pulling the lead player aside on the dock. "We're
  being watched. Two men, cargo handlers, been following us since we cleared
  customs. Third one I haven't placed yet." The Acme fixers have already begun.
