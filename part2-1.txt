================================================================================
ADMIRAL HALL'S BRIEFING — IN CHARACTER
Part 2, Player-Facing Mission Briefing (Voice of Admiral Hall)
================================================================================

The letter arrives folded inside a sealed oilskin pouch, delivered by a
dockside boy who doesn't know who paid him. The handwriting is precise.
The seal is plain wax — no crest.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

You delivered the pouch. Good. I expected nothing less, though I confess the
route you took to get here was rather more dramatic than I had planned for.
We will discuss that another time.

You are in Liege now. That is where I need you to be, and I will not pretend
otherwise. The delivery was the reason. What comes next is the reason I sent
you in the first place.

There is a man in this city. His name is Fenn. He is retired, he is careful,
and he has been waiting a long time for someone I trust to walk through that
harbor. He will find you — or rather, he will arrange to be found. When that
happens, I am asking you to listen to what he has to say. Not because I am
ordering you. I am past the point of ordering you anywhere. But because what
he carries is important, and because you are, at this particular moment, the
only people I know who can move freely enough to be useful.

He will ask you to escort something. A person, most likely, though I will not
confirm that here. The route will take you through waters that are not
comfortable and not well-charted. You have managed worse. The compensation
will be fair. More importantly, the work matters — not to me personally, not
to any one kingdom, but to the larger question of what this world actually is
and what it is going to become.

I know you have questions about that. I know you have seen things on your
journey that do not fit the maps you were raised on. I am not going to answer
those questions in a letter. What I will tell you is this: the world is larger
than the Alliance has told you, older than the histories admit, and
considerably more complicated than either of us would prefer. You are beginning
to understand that. Fenn can help you understand it further, if you let him.

There are people in Liege who will take an interest in your vessel. Some of
them work for institutions you would recognize. Some of them do not. I would
encourage you to keep your stay brief, your cargo secured, and your crew
close. Liege is not hostile to you — not yet — but it is a city that watches,
and you are carrying something that draws attention whether you intend it to
or not.

You know what I mean. You have known since the masthead.

Do not linger. Meet Fenn. Hear the contract. Then move.

I will be in contact when circumstances allow. Until then, trust the work.

— H.

P.S. If anyone asks who sent you, you were hired through a Dagnarius factor
whose name you have already forgotten. You were carrying agricultural survey
documents. You have never heard of me.

END OF DOCUMENT
